04

Good to know

01 What's the minimum order? add

Catering and bulk orders start at $35. Message us your headcount and we'll suggest quantities to fit your group and budget.

02 How far ahead should I order? add

For larger spreads, a day or two of notice helps us source and fry everything fresh. For smaller catering, same-day is often possible — just ask on WhatsApp.

03 Do you deliver across the GTA? add

Yes. We deliver catering across the Greater Toronto Area. Send us your postal code and date on WhatsApp and we'll confirm your area and delivery window.

04 Can you do veg, chicken, and egg together? add

Absolutely. Mix and match across puchkas, vada pav, momos, rolls, chaats, bowls and Maggi. Add chicken, egg, paneer or cheese to power up any item.

05 How do I get bulk pricing? add

Tap any order button to reach us on WhatsApp. Share your headcount, items, and date, and we'll quote bulk pricing and sort delivery in a couple of minutes.
